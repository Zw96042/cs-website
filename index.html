Start curious. Leave capable.

We make room for beginners, ambitious builders, and everyone still figuring out which one they are.

The club is less about knowing the answer and more about learning how to find it.

Members work at their own level, pair up when a problem gets stubborn, and share the reasoning behind what they build. You can arrive with a complete app idea or nothing more than a question.

We care about readable code, useful feedback, and projects that teach something beyond the tutorial.

What happens in the room.

Each meeting creates a clear next step without turning curiosity into homework.

  1. Learn one useful idea.

    A short explanation gives everyone enough context to start, without taking over the meeting.

  2. Build while help is nearby.

    Use the rest of the session to work, ask questions, review code, and make visible progress.

  3. Share how it works.

    Explain the decisions, the failure, and the fix. Understanding grows when it can be communicated.

Ask about the next meeting →
Do I need experience?

No. The first session is designed to give you a useful place to start, even if you have never written code.

What should I bring?

A laptop is useful but not required. We can pair you with another member for the meeting.

Can I bring my own project?

Yes. Bring the project, the problem, or the half-formed idea. Someone can help you find the next step.
